First Meal Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short: First Meal keeps everything on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics and no advertising. We never see your data.

What the app stores

First Meal stores only what you type in and what you log while using it:

  • Your baby's name, date of birth, whether they are a boy or a girl, whether they were born early, and the feeding approach you chose.
  • Your answers to the safety questions: any diagnosed food allergy and which foods, eczema, family history of allergy, and any medical condition or supervised diet you describe.
  • Meals marked as done and your answer for each (all of it, some, refused), reactions you log, days marked as unwell, meal swaps, reminder settings, and the progress of a meal in the guided timer.
  • Recipes you opened recently, for the Search screen, and the meals you prepared in the app without marking them as done, so the plan knows what is in your freezer. These stay on the phone only.

Where it is stored

On your iPhone, and, if iCloud is turned on for the app, in the private part of your own iCloud account so it can sync to your other devices. iCloud is run by Apple under your Apple Account; the data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only your signed-in devices can read it. We have no access to your iCloud and no copy of your data anywhere.

The app works fully without iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for First Meal in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

What leaves your phone

  • Nothing automatically. First Meal has no account system, no server of its own, no analytics, no crash reporting of our own, no advertising and no third-party software that collects data.
  • The doctor's PDF, only when you create it and choose where to send it through the iOS share sheet.
  • A recipe, only when you tap Share and choose where to send it.
  • The shopping list, only when you tap Share or Add to Reminders. A shared list link carries the list itself (dish names, amounts and days, nothing about your baby) inside the link; the page that shows it stores nothing, and the ticks stay on the phone that opened it. Adding the list to Reminders writes it to your own Reminders app, after you allow it.
  • A recipe, only when you tap Share on a recipe. The link opens the recipe on this site; the page shows the recipe and nothing about your baby, and it stores nothing.
  • Source pages. Tapping an information icon opens the official page (NHS, WHO, UNICEF, CDC) in Safari inside the app. Those sites receive an ordinary page request and have their own privacy policies. First Meal sends them nothing about you or your baby.
  • The emergency call button opens the Phone app with your region's emergency number. It never dials by itself.

Notifications

Reminders and the meal timer use notifications scheduled on your phone. They are off until you turn one on, and iOS asks your permission first. No notification is sent from a server.

Children

First Meal is made for parents and carers, not for children. The information you enter about your baby stays with you, on your phone and in your own iCloud. We do not collect it, and we could not identify your child from anything we hold, because we hold nothing.

Health information

Your safety answers and reaction logs are health information about your baby. They are treated like everything else in the app: stored only on your devices and your iCloud, never sent to us. First Meal is built on official guidance and is not medical advice. If you are worried about your baby, talk to your doctor.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all data removes everything from the phone and from your iCloud.
  • Or delete the app from your iPhone, then remove its iCloud data under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → First Meal.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. The app links to this page from Settings.
