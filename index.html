First Meal Support

A daily plan for your baby, from the first taste of solid food to the family table at two.

First Meal turns your baby's age into a plan: how many meals, what to cook, what comes from the freezer, and when a new allergen is introduced. Then it guides each meal from the stove to the high chair and logs it with one question. Every health rule shows its official source. It is not medical advice.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There are no accounts and no servers of ours. See the privacy policy.

Why does the plan start on a Monday?

The plan starts on the first Monday on or after your baby turns six months, and runs in weeks from there. The first week is a single small meal a day.

A new allergen was moved. Why?

Allergens come one a week, at breakfast on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. If a breakfast is refused, or none of that week's breakfasts is marked as done, the allergen tries again the next Monday and the later ones wait. If your baby is unwell, the Unwell button holds new allergens until they are better.

The timer didn't make a sound

The timer sounds in the app, and sends a notification when the app is in the background. Check that notifications are allowed for First Meal in your iPhone's Settings, and that Focus isn't silencing them.

How do I share the log with our doctor?

On Profile, tap the three dots at the top right, then Share with doctor. Choose the period and create the PDF; it opens in the share sheet so you can send it however you like.

Something looks wrong in a recipe

Every recipe is checked against official guidance before it ships, but tell us if something is unclear. Email the recipe name and what you saw.
