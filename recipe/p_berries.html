From 9 months

Quartered berries

4 min · Makes 1 portion

Allergens

No common allergens

How to serve

Spoon-fed: Mash with a fork.

Baby-led: Offer the quarters.

Ingredients · 2 items

  • 3Strawberries
  • 10Blueberries

Method · 2 steps

  1. Wash the berries.
  2. Cut every berry lengthways into quarters.
