From 12 months

Shakshuka with toast fingers

35 min · Makes 2 adults and a toddler, with leftovers · Iron-rich

Allergens

Contains: egg, wheat

How to serve

Baby-led: Offer chopped egg and toast fingers to dip.

Ingredients · 9 items

  • 4Egg
  • 800 gChopped tomatoes, tinned (no added salt)
  • 2Red pepper
  • 1Onion
  • 2 clovesGarlic
  • 1 tspGround cumin
  • 1 tspMild paprika
  • 1 tbspOlive oil
  • 4 slicesWholemeal bread

Method · 4 steps

  1. Soften the sliced onion and peppers in the oil for 10 minutes. Add the garlic and spices.
  2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes until thick.
  3. Make 4 dips and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook for 8-10 minutes until the whites and yolks are fully set.
  4. Serve your baby's egg chopped with sauce and toast fingers; season the family's at the table.
  5. Take your baby's portion out here, before any salt.
