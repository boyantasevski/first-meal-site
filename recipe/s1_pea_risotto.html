From 6 months

Pea and courgette risotto

30 min · Makes 4 portions

Allergens

Contains: milk

How to serve

Spoon-fed: Mash or blend to the texture your baby is used to.

Baby-led: Let it cool and shape into soft balls.

Ingredients · 7 items

  • 100 gRisotto rice
  • 1Courgette
  • 80 gFrozen peas
  • 1/2Onion
  • 1 tbspOlive oil
  • 1 tbspFull-fat cream cheese (pasteurised)
  • 600 mlWater

Method · 4 steps

  1. Soften the chopped onion in the oil for 5 minutes. Stir in the rice for 1 minute.
  2. Add the water a ladle at a time, stirring, for 20-25 minutes until the rice is very soft. No stock: it is too salty.
  3. Stir in the grated courgette and peas for the last 5 minutes, then the cream cheese.
  4. Mash lightly so the peas are squashed.

Cool leftover rice within 1 hour, keep it in the fridge and use it within 24 hours.
