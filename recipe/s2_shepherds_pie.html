From 9 months

Lamb and lentil shepherd's pie

55 min · Makes 6 portions · Freezes well · Iron-rich

Allergens

Contains: milk

How to serve

Spoon-fed: Chop into small soft pieces, or mash roughly if lumps are still new.

Baby-led: Offer soft forkfuls, or press into small cakes.

Freezing

Freezes well.

Ingredients · 10 items

  • 250 gLamb mince
  • 100 gGreen or brown lentils, cooked
  • 1Onion
  • 1Carrot
  • 1 tbspTomato purée (no added salt)
  • pinchFresh or dried thyme
  • 300 mlWater
  • 500 gPotato
  • 15 gUnsalted butter
  • 50 mlWhole cows' milk

Method · 4 steps

  1. Brown the lamb, breaking it up finely, until there is no pink. Spoon off any fat.
  2. Add the chopped onion and carrot, the lentils, purée, thyme and water. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Mash the lentils into the sauce with a fork.
  4. Boil the peeled potato for 15 minutes and mash with the butter and milk. Spread over the meat and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until piping hot.
