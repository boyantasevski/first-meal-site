From 9 months

Butter bean and vegetable stew

35 min · Makes 5 portions · Freezes well · Iron-rich

Allergens

No common allergens

How to serve

Spoon-fed: Chop into small soft pieces, or mash roughly if lumps are still new.

Baby-led: Offer soft vegetable pieces and squashed beans; it is thick enough to scoop with fingers.

Freezing

Freezes well.

Ingredients · 9 items

  • 1 tin, drained (240 g)Butter beans, tinned (no added salt)
  • 1Carrot
  • 1Courgette
  • 1/2Red pepper
  • 1Onion
  • 1 cloveGarlic
  • 1/2 tspMild paprika
  • 400 gChopped tomatoes, tinned (no added salt)
  • 1 tbspOlive oil

Method · 3 steps

  1. Soften the chopped onion, garlic, carrot and pepper in the oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the courgette, paprika, tomatoes and beans. Simmer for 20 minutes until the vegetables are soft.
  3. Squash every bean flat with a fork before serving.
