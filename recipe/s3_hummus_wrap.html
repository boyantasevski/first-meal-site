Hummus and roasted vegetable wrap

25 min · Makes 2 portions · Iron-rich

Allergens

Contains: sesame, wheat

How to serve

Baby-led: Offer the pieces.

Ingredients · 7 items

  • 1Wholemeal tortilla wrap
  • 60 gChickpeas, tinned (no added salt)
  • 1 tspTahini (sesame paste)
  • 1/2 tspLemon juice
  • 1 tbspOlive oil
  • 1/2Courgette
  • 1/2Red pepper

Method · 3 steps

  1. Roast the courgette and pepper strips in half the oil at 200°C for 20 minutes until soft.
  2. Blend the chickpeas, tahini, lemon and the rest of the oil into a smooth hummus.
  3. Spread over the wrap, add the vegetables, roll up, and cut into short narrow pieces.
