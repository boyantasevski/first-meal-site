First Spoon · Recipe

From 9 months

Tofu and vegetable noodles

25 min · Makes 3 portions · Iron-rich

Allergens

Contains: sesame, soya, wheat

How to serve

Spoon-fed: Chop into small soft pieces, or mash roughly if lumps are still new.

Baby-led: Offer tofu fingers, soft vegetable strips and short noodles.

Ingredients · 10 items

  • 150 gFirm tofu
  • 100 gWheat noodles (egg-free)
  • 100 gBroccoli
  • 1/2Red pepper
  • 1Carrot
  • 1 cloveGarlic
  • 1/2 tsp, gratedFresh ginger
  • 1 tspTahini (sesame paste)
  • 1 tspLemon juice
  • 1 tbspRapeseed oil

Method · 4 steps

  1. Cut the tofu into fingers and fry in half the oil until golden on each side.
  2. Cut the vegetables into thin strips and steam the carrot for 5 minutes first. Stir-fry everything in the rest of the oil with the garlic and ginger for 6-8 minutes until each piece squashes easily between your finger and thumb. Add a splash of water if needed.
  3. Cook the noodles until soft and cut into short lengths.
  4. Mix the tahini with the lemon juice and 1 tbsp water and stir it through. No soy sauce for the baby.
