From 9 months

Mini beef meatballs in tomato sauce

35 min · Makes 20 small meatballs (5 portions) · Freezes well · Iron-rich

Allergens

Contains: egg

oats (may contain gluten)

How to serve

Spoon-fed: Mash the meatballs into the sauce.

Baby-led: Cut each meatball in half or into quarters so the pieces are soft and small.

Freezing

Freezes well, in the sauce.

Ingredients · 10 items

  • 300 gLean beef mince
  • 20 gPorridge oats
  • 1Egg
  • 1/2, gratedOnion
  • 1/2, gratedCourgette
  • 1/2 tspDried oregano
  • 400 gChopped tomatoes, tinned (no added salt)
  • 1 cloveGarlic
  • a few leavesFresh basil
  • 1 tbspOlive oil

Method · 4 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the mince, oats, egg, onion, courgette and oregano.
  2. Roll into about 20 small balls and bake on a lined tray for 18-20 minutes until cooked through with no pink.
  3. Meanwhile soften the garlic in the oil, add the tomatoes and basil, and simmer for 15 minutes. Blend smooth.
  4. Add the meatballs to the sauce.
