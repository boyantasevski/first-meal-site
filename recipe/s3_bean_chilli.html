From 12 months

Sweet potato and bean chilli

40 min · Makes 2 adults and a toddler, with leftovers · Freezes well · Iron-rich

Allergens

Contains: milk

How to serve

Baby-led: Offer on a preloaded spoon, or mixed into soft rice balls.

Freezing

Freezes well.

Ingredients · 12 items

  • 1 tin, drained (240 g)Black beans, tinned (no added salt)
  • 1 tin, drained (240 g)Kidney beans, tinned (no added salt)
  • 400 gSweet potato
  • 1Red pepper
  • 1Onion
  • 2 clovesGarlic
  • 1 tspGround cumin
  • 1 tspMild paprika
  • 800 gChopped tomatoes, tinned (no added salt)
  • 1 tbspOlive oil
  • 250 gRice
  • to serveFull-fat plain yoghurt

Method · 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice in plenty of water until soft, as the packet says.
  2. Soften the onion, pepper and garlic in the oil for 8 minutes. Stir in the spices.
  3. Add the diced sweet potato, tomatoes and beans. Simmer for 25 minutes until the potato is soft.
  4. Take out your baby's portion and squash every bean flat.
  5. Take your baby's portion out here, before any salt.
  6. Add chilli and salt to the family's pot if you like. Serve with rice and yoghurt.

Cool leftover rice within 1 hour, keep it in the fridge and use it within 24 hours.
