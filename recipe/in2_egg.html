From 6 months

Egg and sweet potato mash

18 min · Makes 1 portion · Iron-rich

Allergens

Contains: egg

How to serve

Spoon-fed: Mash until smooth; loosen with a little cooled boiled water or baby's usual milk.

Baby-led: Let it cool and offer on a preloaded spoon.

First time with egg

First time: about 1/4 teaspoon of the mashed egg, mixed into the sweet potato. Next time: 1/2 teaspoon. Then build up to the full amount over the next few days. Offer it at breakfast so you can watch your baby for the next 2 hours. Some reactions to egg come later, up to a few days afterwards: a rash, an eczema flare, vomiting, tummy pain or loose stools. If you see them, stop giving egg and talk to your doctor.

Ingredients · 2 items

  • 1Egg
  • 60 gSweet potato

Method · 3 steps

  1. Boil the egg for 10 minutes so it is hard all the way through; cool it in cold water and peel.
  2. Steam or boil the diced sweet potato for 12-15 minutes until very soft.
  3. Mash the egg finely and mix it into the mashed sweet potato.
